# NXLimit Campaign Commission Policy

**Version:** 2026-08-12  
**Applies to:** Brand & Growth campaigns and partnerships  
**Effective date:** Upon written acknowledgement by the employee and approval by the company  

This document sets out how campaign revenue is attributed, reconciled and commissioned for NXLimit’s Brand & Growth role. It is designed to be simple enough to explain in one sentence, specific enough to avoid arguments, and aligned with the way revenue actually flows through our systems.

---

## 1. Commission rates by sale type

| Sale type | Commission | Cap / accelerator |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Tracked digital program sales 数字训练计划 | **10%** of net collected revenue | **13%** on the portion above CNY 80,000 in a single Shanghai calendar month |
| Online 1:1 coaching from the campaign 线上1对1私教 | **8%** of net collected revenue | First three paid months only |
| In-person package from the campaign 线下训练包 | **5%** of net collected revenue | First paid package only |
| Team / institution contract 团队/机构合同 | Pre-approved written flat fee | Agreed before the opportunity is approved |
| Presentation / workshop / small camp / training camp 演讲/工作坊/短期营/训练营 | Pre-approved written flat fee | Agreed before the opportunity is approved |

### Monthly performance bonus stays separate
The existing monthly performance bonus rewards execution, content quality, systems and reporting. Commission rewards measurable revenue results. The two are never mixed.

---

## 2. What counts as commissionable revenue

**Net collected revenue** = cash actually collected from customers, minus only:

- Refunds and chargebacks
- Applicable VAT / taxes
- Payment and platform fees attributable to the order
- Pre-approved partner revenue share
- Pre-approved advertising and direct fulfilment costs tied to the campaign

We do **not** deduct general company overhead, founder salaries, rent or unrelated software subscriptions.

---

## 3. Attribution rules

These rules decide whether a sale belongs to a campaign.

1. **Coupon or referral code takes priority.** If the customer uses the campaign’s code, the sale is attributed to that campaign.
2. **Tracked link or QR code:** the attribution window is **60 days** from the first click.
3. **Lead source field in the CRM:** if a lead is uploaded or tagged before follow-up begins, and the campaign demonstrably brought them back after 90+ days of dormancy, the sale can be reactivated credit.
4. **Existing active leads do not become campaign revenue** just because they saw a later post.
5. **Orders must be paid and outside the 14-day refund period** before they count.
6. **Cancelled, refunded or fraudulent orders earn no commission.**
7. **Renewals and existing customers** need an explicit rule recorded in the campaign file.
8. **Brand-awareness campaigns without trackable leads** count toward the monthly performance score, not revenue commission.
9. **Manual attribution** requires evidence and approval from both the employee and the company.

### Attribution share
A campaign may involve more than one person. The default is 100% to the Brand & Growth owner. When roles are split, the company assigns the share before launch:

| Role | Suggested share |
|------|----------------|
| Proposed / originated the campaign | 40% |
| Managed and executed it | 40% |
| Closed the customer or partnership | 20% |

If one person performs all three roles, the share is 100%.

## 4. Examples

### Example A — Digital program campaign, one month above threshold

- Customer payments collected: CNY 100,000
- Refunds and approved direct costs/fees: CNY 0
- Net collected revenue: CNY 100,000
- Calculation:
  - First CNY 80,000 × 10% = CNY 8,000
  - Remaining CNY 20,000 × 13% = CNY 2,600
  - **Commission = CNY 10,600**

### Example B — Digital program campaign, 80% attribution share

- Net collected revenue: CNY 100,000
- Employee proposed and managed the campaign; founder closed the customer
- Attribution share: 80%
- Calculation:
  - CNY 10,600 × 80% = **CNY 8,480**

### Example C — Online 1:1 coaching

- New client pays CNY 5,000/month for 4 months through the campaign
- Only the first 3 months are eligible: CNY 15,000
- Commission: CNY 15,000 × 8% = **CNY 1,200**

### Example D — In-person package

- New client buys a CNY 8,000 in-person package
- Commission: CNY 8,000 × 5% = **CNY 400**

### Example E — Team contract with pre-approved flat fee

- Company closes a CNY 500,000 team contract
- Pre-approved finder’s fee: CNY 15,000
- **Commission = CNY 15,000** regardless of contract size

## 5. Approval and reconciliation workflow

1. **Propose.** The employee submits the campaign or partnership in Company Operations, including budget, tracking method and expected product type.
2. **Approve.** The company approves the commission rate, attribution window, attribution share and any flat fee.
3. **Track.** The system generates a campaign ID, QR code, referral code and tracked links.
4. **Collect.** Orders and payments flow automatically into the campaign ledger from Postgres.
5. **Reconcile.** Monthly, the employee and company review:
   - Total revenue collected
   - Refunds and direct costs
   - Net collected revenue
   - Eligible revenue
   - Attribution share
   - Commission owed
6. **Dispute.** The employee has three working days to question missing orders or incorrect attribution.
7. **Lock.** The approved commission is locked and included in the next payroll statement.
8. **Adjust.** Later refunds are shown as a separate adjustment — never deducted invisibly or from base salary.

## 7. Important notes

- Terms should not be changed retrospectively for campaigns already approved.

- The 13% accelerator applies only to revenue above CNY 80,000 within a single Shanghai calendar month. It is **not** retroactive to the whole month.
